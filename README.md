# ⚡ Zapier Business Workflow Automation

> **Practical CRM, lead management, and business workflow automation using Zapier, HubSpot, Google Sheets, Gmail, and workflow logic.**

## 📌 Project Overview

I built this **Zapier Business Workflow Automation** project to demonstrate how repetitive business processes can be connected and automated from **lead capture to CRM updates, data processing, notifications, and follow-up**.

The workflow is designed around a simple business goal:

**Capture leads → Clean data → Qualify → Route → Update CRM → Notify → Follow Up**

Instead of manually moving information between forms, spreadsheets, CRM systems, and email, the workflow connects these steps into one repeatable automation process.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Many businesses still spend valuable time:

* Copying leads into CRM systems
* Updating Google Sheets manually
* Cleaning contact information
* Checking whether leads are qualified
* Assigning leads to team members
* Sending follow-up notifications
* Tracking lead status across multiple tools

This creates delays, duplicate work, inconsistent data, and missed follow-ups.

### 💡 Automation Solution

I designed a practical Zapier workflow that automates the movement of lead information across connected business tools.

**Lead Capture → Validation → Formatting → Qualification → CRM / Sheets Sync → Routing → Notification → Follow-Up**

This approach follows real business automation patterns such as lead capture, qualification, routing, CRM updates, spreadsheet synchronization, and sales handoff.
